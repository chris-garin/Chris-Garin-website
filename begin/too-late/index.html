Someone Has Already Done It.

“Someone has already done it.”

Chris Garin

I open LinkedIn and someone has already done the exact thing I was about to do. Done it well. With a bigger audience than mine. And the doubt lands instantly: it has been done, the good spots are gone, why bother.

The market is not a set of chairs

Attention is not a finite row of seats that fill up and then it is over. New people arrive every day who have never heard the thing said in your words, from your angle, by someone who sounds like you. The person who is already famous for it does not reach them. You might.

Your angle is the moat

I am a full-stack developer with eleven years in marketing, building the product, the audience, and the distribution at the same time. That specific combination is mine. Someone can copy my topic in an afternoon. They cannot copy the exact path that got me here or the way I see the problem because of it.

That is the part that does not get taken. The topic was never the scarce thing. Your version of it is.

It changes nothing about today

James Clear, the author of Atomic Habits, would take the emotion out of this completely. Outcomes are lagging indicators of habits. Whether someone did it first is a story about them. It does not change my job today, which is to show up, ship the next one, and make it slightly better than the last.

Reps compound, comparison does not

Get 1% better every time you publish, and a hundred posts later you are almost three times better than when you started. The more talented person who posts when they feel like it, or who is frozen by comparison, stays roughly where they were. Being second is fine. Being later is fine. Execution and persistence beat an original topic almost every time.

So my competition is not the person who already did it. It is my own consistency.

Decide who you are, then prove it

Clear also builds habits around identity. I do not have to think “I am competing with the people who got here first.” I can think “I am the kind of person who keeps showing up and shipping something useful.” Every post is a vote for that person. Nobody else can cast those votes for me.

The takeaway: someone has already done it, and that was never the real question. The real question is whether I show up today. Late to the topic, first to my version of it, and consistent enough to let it compound.
