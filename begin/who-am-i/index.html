I Haven’t Made Real Money Yet. Who Am I to Talk?

“I haven’t made real money yet. Who am I to talk?”

Chris Garin

This doubt shows up every single time I open the camera. Nothing I have built has printed real revenue yet, and a voice in the back of my head says the same thing every time: who are you to talk about any of this? Wait until you have actually won something. Then you can speak.

But the premise is wrong. I am not selling a finished result. I am documenting a build while it is happening, in real time, with my name and face on it. Those are two completely different jobs. One needs you to have arrived. The other only needs you to be honestly walking.

The honest number beats the polished one

Here is the story I keep coming back to. I had a dashboard that proudly reported a 75% reply rate on my outreach. It was reading the wrong field. When I fixed it, the real number was 13%. I corrected my own metric down, from a flattering lie to an ugly truth, on purpose, because the true number was more useful to me.

That correction is worth more than the fake win ever was. People do not trust the person with the perfect chart. They trust the person who will show you the ugly one and tell you exactly how they got it wrong.

You are allowed to teach from the trench

You do not need to be at the top of the mountain. You only need to be one step further up the trail than the person reading, and willing to describe what the climb actually looks like from where you are standing. Real, unfinished, in progress. That is not a weaker position. For most people it is the only one they will believe.

The takeaway: you do not need proof that you have arrived. You need proof that you are actually walking. That, you already have.
