How a Realtor's Postcards Beat His Email Funnel

Chris Garin • August 28, 2026 • 1 min read

A realtor I work with pulls 3x more replies from postcards than from his entire email automation setup.

It's the lowest-tech thing he does, and it's his best-performing channel.

What's crazy is, I have another realtor client who's never mailed a postcard in her life. Her whole thing is just being active on Facebook and Instagram.

But when she's on social, she doesn't think of it as a task she has to get out of the way. She shows up in the comments like an actual friend.

Remembers the kid's name. Asks how the trip went. Actually genuinely asks how they are. Treats a potential client like a person she already knows.

Two clients. Opposite playbooks. One mails paper, one lives in the replies. Same result.

So it's not the postcards. And it's not the comments, and emails. It's that both of them are doing the one thing you can't automate: paying attention to one person at a time.

That doesn't scale. That's the entire point.

AI just made it trivial to fake attention at infinite scale.

The personalized email that isn't personal. The comment a bot could've written.

Which is exactly why the real version is about to be worth more than all of it.

The stuff that doesn't scale used to be the consolation prize. Now it's the moat.
